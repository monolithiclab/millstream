Millstream

A calm instrument for work that flows: warm paper, one deep millrace teal, and every item a pebble carried along a gentle channel.

Design system 0.2 · light first · 15 Oct 2026 · by Monolithic Lab

Palette. A warm paper ground and soft white cards replace the cold grey. Ink is a warm near-black. One accent, deep millrace teal, still means "flowing": running work, live data, the primary action. Status colours are muted, earthy versions of the usual set, each paired with a shape and a word.

Type. Instrument Serif brings the editorial voice: page titles, step names in panels, and the big numbers people glance at. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next stays for everything you read or click, and its Mono only for ids, JSON and logs. All three are open-licensed and bundled.

The signature: channels and pebbles

Streams are soft channels whose width follows volume. Items in flight are round pebbles drifting along; a queue is pebbles gathering at the next step. Filters are weirs, a gentle gate that lets part of the flow through.
