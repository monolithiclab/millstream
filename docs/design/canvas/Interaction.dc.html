Interaction notes

How the UI moves, listens and answers. Live but calm: data changes in place, motion carries meaning, and every action says what it will do before it does it.

Live updates

  • Server-sent events arrive at about 4 per second per pipeline. The client coalesces them and paints at most every 250 ms.
  • Numbers update in place: tabular figures, fixed-width slots, right-aligned. No count-up tweens. A changed value gets a 600 ms soft tint behind it, never a flash.
  • New rows never push the list while someone reads it. Scrolled to the top, rows slide in (120 ms). Scrolled down, a pill appears: "12 new · show".
  • Lost connection: after 2 s, the Live mark becomes "Reconnecting · 4 s" (dashed outline). After 5 s stale, live numbers dim to 60 %. On reconnect, fetch a snapshot, then resume the stream.
  • Live logs mark a gap when the stream drops: a dashed rule with the missing window, "06:21:04 → 06:21:19".
  • Engine failover (~30 s): a read-only banner; action buttons disable with the reason as tooltip. Nothing is queued behind the user's back.

Motion in the graph

  • Grains drift along channels at a constant 24 px/s, linear. Grain spacing reflects items in flight (one grain per ~14 px at most), so a busy channel looks busy at the same speed.
  • A queue is grains packed against the head of the next step, up to 12, then a number. Growth is visible without reading anything.
  • Problems never blink. A failing step gets a 2 px border and a filled triangle; a slow step gets a badge. Change of state is one 120 ms cross-fade.
  • Animation runs only for channels in the viewport and only while the tab is visible.
  • prefers-reduced-motion: grains freeze at their last position, packed queues stay, panels open without sliding.
  • Overlays (Flow, Errors, Latency, Queues) recolour labels and channels; layout never moves. 25-step graphs auto-layout left to right (ELK), fit on open, show a minimap above 12 steps, and switch to compact nodes when zoom would put text under 11 px.

Single keys never fire inside inputs. Hints sit next to the buttons they trigger and can be hidden in settings; single-key shortcuts can be turned off (WCAG 2.1.4).

Focus order

  • Skip link → main navigation → page header (title, state, actions) → main content → side panel.
  • Graph: Tab visits steps in topological order (left to right, then top to bottom); arrow keys follow edges. Enter opens the side panel and moves focus to its heading; Esc closes it and returns focus to the step.
  • Dialogs trap focus. Destructive dialogs open on Cancel; others on the primary action. Every dialog states its consequence in the body, and its button repeats the verb and count ("Replay 2 items").
  • Inbox: after a decision, focus lands on the next item's title, and a polite live region says "Approved. 8 left."
  • Focus ring: 2 px accent outside a 2 px ground gap, both themes, never removed.

Language, numbers, scale

  • French runs about 30 % longer: buttons hug their label, nothing has a fixed text width, toolbars wrap. Only ids and URLs truncate, in the middle (it_7f3a…9c21), with the full value on hover and copy.
  • Numbers through Intl.NumberFormat: 1,240 in English, 1 240 in French (narrow no-break space). Money is USD with cents: $0.02 and 0,02 $.
  • Durations pick one or two units: 8 ms, 2.4 s, 3 min 12 s, 2 d 4 h (FR: 2 j 4 h). Times under 24 h are relative in lists and absolute on hover.
  • Tables are virtualised with fixed 36 px rows; filters run server-side with CEL and always show "31 of 50,214".
  • Timelines aggregate per step; a step row expands into a density strip of its 2,000 deliveries, and a bin opens the items behind it.
  • Errors up to 4 KB show three lines, then "Show full error · 3.6 KB" and Copy.
